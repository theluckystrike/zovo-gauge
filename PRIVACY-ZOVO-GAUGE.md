# Privacy notice

Gauge reads visible video titles, like counts, view counts, and upload ages on the
supported video site. It calculates engagement locally and keeps observations in
`chrome.storage.session`, which is cleared when the browser session ends. Filter
settings and a successful licence record are stored locally on the device.

No observed title, engagement score, browsing ledger, or filter setting is sent
to a developer server. When a user chooses Activate, the entered licence key is
sent to the licence-verification endpoint solely to validate the purchase. The
extension does not use an external dislike-data service.

Privacy policy: https://zovo.one/privacy

Complete corresponding source: https://github.com/theluckystrike/zovo-gauge
